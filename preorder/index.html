How pre-orders work

Everything in the Key Bored store is a pre-order. We make each batch to order, so what you buy today ships when production finishes. Current estimate: Spring 2027 (estimated).

The timeline

  1. Pre-order windowChoose your OneKey and place your order while the window is open.
  2. ProductionWhen the window closes, we order parts in the exact quantities ordered and start making them.
  3. Quality checkEvery unit is assembled, clicked, and checked by hand.
  4. ShippingOrders ship in batches with tracking by email.

Questions

When am I charged?

At checkout, when you place your pre-order.

Can I cancel?

Yes, for a full refund, any time before production starts.

Will the ship date change?

Ship dates are estimates. If anything moves, we email every customer with the new timeline and the reason.

Can I combine items?

Yes. Everything in one pre-order ships together.

New batches, new colors, and the occasional story. No spam.

Our story

Made to a standard

Most things on a desk are made to hit a price. We would rather make one thing well: a single mechanical key, built like the keyboards people spend hundreds on, and small enough to carry.

Every OneKey uses a real switch, a real keycap, and materials chosen for how they sound and feel. Click it while you think. Carry it as a reminder of what a well-made object feels like.
